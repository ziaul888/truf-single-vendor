About this ground

Our most-booked 5-a-side pitch. FIFA-grade artificial turf with under-soil drainage — playable through monsoon. Full floodlight coverage for evening matches and a rebound wall for warm-ups. Sits at the front of the venue, closest to parking and the locker rooms.
